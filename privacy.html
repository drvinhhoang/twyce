Twyce Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Twyce is a camera app. It records one take and makes a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file from it. This policy explains what data the app collects and why.

Your videos, photos, and sound stay on your device

Twyce does not upload your videos, photos, sound, or prompter scripts. The app uses the camera and the microphone only to record. It saves your finished files to your photo library with add-only access, so it cannot read your other photos.

Information we collect

We collect: device model, iOS version, app version, crash reports, anonymous usage statistics, and subscription status. If you allow tracking, we also use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts. Apple processes all payments. We never see your payment details.

How we use this information

  • To fix defects and crashes.
  • To improve the app.
  • To measure which ads bring people to the app.

Data sharing

Google Firebase processes this data for us. Its privacy terms are at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not sell your data.

Tracking

Twyce asks for permission to track. If you select "Ask App Not to Track", the app works the same, and we do not use the advertising identifier. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary to fix defects, improve the app, or meet legal duties.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete your data, withdraw your consent, or object to processing. To do this, email us at the address below. Deleting the app also resets the app's analytics ID.

Children

Twyce is not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

International users

Our service providers can process data in countries other than your country. Where the law requires it, they protect the data with standard safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update this page and the effective date.
